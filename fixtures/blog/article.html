Swapping our summariser to a cheaper tier

We moved the nightly digest job off the large tier six weeks ago and it held. Median latency dropped from 4.2s to 1.8s per document.

The one failure mode we hit was a dropped bullet on documents over roughly 8k tokens, which we fixed by chunking:

for chunk in chunks(doc, max_tokens=6000):
    summarise(chunk)

Six weeks in, no regressions in the review queue.
