X PLAYBOOK (on top of the hard rules above)

Who reads it: engineers who want a day-one verdict and a number they can
argue with. Fast, direct, sceptical of vendor claims.

Shape (formats `post`, `reply`): the count and what it was counted over in
the first line, and that first line stands alone; then short paragraphs with
a blank line between them; one proof report; one specific question an
engineer can answer. Within the length rule. A short list of lines is fine
(three or four points, one per line). Never the engagement-bait shape common
on X: an all-caps opener, a "here's how" teaser, a promise of a secret.

Thread (format `thread`): parts separated by a line holding only `---`, each
within the length rule. Part 1 is the hook with the headline count. Then one
part per week or point, each with its count and at most one proof report.
Last part: the question.

The link: put the model page link in `first_comment` (it is posted as the
first reply), not in the post, unless the instructions say to test the link
in the post. Hashtags: none (in our sample of X posts about AI models, those
with a hashtag got about half the likes per view).
