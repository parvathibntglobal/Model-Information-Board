Why the invoice is often higher than the list price

AI coding tools bill in three ways. A flat seat costs the same however much you use it. A credit pool includes a set amount of usage and charges for more. Usage limits cap how much you can use in a time window, and the fix is usually a higher plan.

Flat seat

Same price every month. Easy to budget. Heavy users cost the same as light ones.

Credit pool

Includes some usage. Past that, you pay per request or credit. Heavy agent use can multiply the bill.

Usage limits

You hit a cap and wait for the reset, or move to a higher plan. Cost rises in steps.

Do AI coding tools charge per token?

Most plans for people charge a flat monthly price with usage limits or an included credit pool. Paying per token is common when you use a model through its API directly, and some plans charge extra usage at API-like rates once the included amount runs out.

Is yearly billing worth it?

Yearly billing is usually cheaper per month, but it locks seats in for a year. Teams still trying tools often start monthly and switch once usage settles.
