Developer analytics security for AI coding teams

What protects the console and the data, and what is not built yet.

Developer analytics security in Kite Pulse rests on email and password sign-in with lockout, access roles the server enforces, an audit log of sign-ins and admin changes, and data that goes only to a server your team runs. Secrets are masked twice and uploads are encrypted in transit. There is no sign-in through a company identity provider, no second sign-in step, no app-level encryption of stored data and no certification.

Sign-in, passwords and account lockout policy

Built-in rules. Compare them with your own account lockout policy best practices.

Sign-in
Email and password. Sessions last 2 hours, or 7 days with "Keep me signed in". Changing a password signs out other devices.
Password rules
At least 12 characters with upper case, lower case, a digit and a symbol, for passwords people choose at sign-up, change or reset.
First sign-in
The first admin, and any account whose password an admin has reset, must change it at next sign-in.
Lockout
An account locks after 10 wrong passwords in 15 minutes. A network address is blocked after 20.
Reset
Password reset by email from the sign-in page, once email is set up on the server.
Who can join
On Login & Registration: admin-created accounts (the default), sign-up by company email domain, or open sign-up with admin approval.

Role based access control in the dashboard

Three access roles: Admin sees everyone, Manager sees themselves and their whole reporting line, Developer sees their own pages. The server enforces this, not just the screen.

  • Custom RBAC roles can be defined on the grid but cannot yet be assigned to accounts.
  • Accounts & Roles: create accounts and set access role, work role and manager. Account management can be delegated to a manager for their own team.

An audit log for admin actions and sign-ins

The Audit Log records sign-ins, failed sign-ins, lockouts and admin changes, over 100 kinds of action. It is read-only and filterable. Passwords and codes never appear in it.

Safeguards for developers

The full list, with what is read on each computer, is on the privacy page. The ones a security review asks about:

  • No server address is built in. The program runs only once it is given your team's own server.
  • No session content uploads until the developer confirms their projects, and an approver signs off where required.
  • Updates come only from your own server, checked for size and integrity before install.

Controls for admins

  • Export or erase one person's data. Erasing anonymises the account, deletes their raw activity records and destroys their encryption keys. Plain-text working copies of their sessions stay on the server. Both actions are recorded in the Audit Log.
  • Retention: set how long plan-usage history, raw content, derived data and audit history are kept. Deletion applies once the server operator enables it.
  • Infrastructure checks, on by default, include repeated failed sign-ins and old collector versions.
  • HTTPS is set up by the automated server install and can be required on any install. Where the server runs is on self-hosted deployment.

What Kite Pulse does not do

Plain answers for a security questionnaire.

  • No sign-in through a company identity provider. Sign-in is email and password.
  • No second sign-in step, such as a code from a phone app.
  • No app-level encryption of stored data. Uploads are encrypted in transit only.
  • No security certification or third-party audit report.
  • No automatic scan for leaked credentials. The Leaked Credentials page fills only after a server operator runs a scan from the command line, and statuses are set by hand.
  • Releases are not signed yet. Updates are checked for size and integrity.
  • No hosted version run by the vendor.

Security questions

Can people sign in to Kite Pulse through our company identity provider?

No. Sign-in is email and password only, and there is no second sign-in step such as a one-time code. Passwords are stored as secure hashes and must meet the password rules.

What happens after repeated wrong passwords?

An account locks after 10 wrong passwords in 15 minutes, and a network address is blocked after 20. Both are recorded in the Audit Log.

Can a manager see developers outside their team?

No. A manager sees themselves and their whole reporting line, including indirect reports. The server enforces this, not just the screen. Admins can change what each role sees per page on Roles & Permissions.

What does the audit log record, and how long is it kept?

Sign-ins, failed sign-ins, lockouts and admin changes, over 100 kinds of action, including exports and erasures of a person's data. It is kept indefinitely by default; a retention period, 365 days by default, applies once the server operator enables deletion.

Is stored data encrypted?

Not by the app. Uploads are encrypted in transit, and stored sessions are scrubbed of secrets. Protection of stored data comes from your server's own disk encryption, or S3 on AWS.

Does Kite Pulse hold a security certification?

No. Kite Pulse holds no security certification or third-party audit report. The server runs on infrastructure your team chooses, described on the self-hosted page.
