An AI spend governance checklist for your first month

AI spend governance starts with three decisions: the budget unit, whether budgets follow teams or projects, and how often someone looks. Pick yours and copy the list.

Checklist builder

AI coding spend: first-month checklist

  1. List each AI coding tool's seat price; an admin enters them on Hourly Rates & Seat Prices.
  2. Decide who in finance needs console access. Tool Accounts is admin by default; admins can grant pages to managers on Roles & Permissions.
  3. Set a monthly budget per team in estimated US dollars on AI Budgets, with alerts at 80% and 100%. Unpriced tokens, usually Codex, Cursor and Copilot, are reported separately.
  4. Ask an admin to schedule the Company report monthly, as PDF or CSV. Email must be configured on the server.
  5. Each month, open Team AI Usage: tokens and estimated cost per tool, and the Models tab for adoption.
  6. Each month, check Tool Accounts for Shared accounts and compare accounts in use with your invoiced seats.
  7. Reconcile with invoices: the estimate is list price, not billed spend.
  8. Before renewing Claude plans, read the Plan usage report: weeks at 80% or more and at the limit.

What Kite Pulse shows you, and what it does not

Figures come from AI coding tools' own session records, on a server your team runs.

What it shows

  • Token use per tool, model, project, developer and day
  • Estimated list-price cost per tool, where a price is known
  • Seat prices you enter, beside usage, for admins
  • Accounts per tool, with shared accounts marked
  • Monthly budgets per team or project, with alerts
  • Claude plan usage per developer, for Claude Code

What it does not show

  • What you were billed: invoices, discounts and credits
  • One total of AI spend across tools
  • Chat assistant seats, API bills or AI tools outside the ten
  • Seats nobody uses: an idle seat never appears in a session
  • A return-on-investment figure

For AI spend across every vendor and invoice, your finance or procurement system is the right place.

Questions from executives and finance

How do I see what my company spends on AI coding tools?

Put token use per tool, an estimated cost from list prices and your seat prices side by side. Kite Pulse reads the session history ten AI coding tools keep on developers' computers and shows token use per tool, model, project and day, with an estimated cost per tool labelled not billed spend. Your invoices remain the record of what you paid.

Does Kite Pulse show our total AI spend?

No. Estimated cost is shown per tool and never added up, because Codex, Cursor and GitHub Copilot usually have no price and many seats are flat subscriptions. Chat assistants, API bills and other company AI spend are outside Kite Pulse altogether.

Can finance see AI cost per developer?

Only admins can, on that developer's AI Usage tab. Managers see token use for their own reporting line, and budgets are set per team or project, never per person.

Does Kite Pulse find unused AI seats?

No. It only sees accounts that appear in AI coding sessions, so a seat nobody uses never shows up. Tool Accounts lists the accounts in use, with plan tier, last seen and email domain, and marks shared ones; compare that list with the seats you pay for.

Can finance get a regular AI usage report by email?

Yes, if email is configured on the server. An admin can schedule the Company report daily, weekly or monthly as PDF or CSV, with a preview and a send history.
